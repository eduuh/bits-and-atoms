## Blog Content Strategy

### Core Principle: Indirect Value First

This blog exists to create **indirect career value** - job opportunities, consulting leads, network building, and professional reputation. Ad revenue and sponsorships are secondary.

### Content Pillars

**1. Problem-Solving Posts**
- Document solutions to real problems you encounter
- Target searchable queries developers Google
- Include context, not just code snippets
- Examples: "Fixing X error in Next.js", "How to configure Cloudflare Pages with custom domains"

**2. Decision & Thinking Posts**
- Explain *why* you made technical decisions, not just *what*
- Show trade-offs and alternatives considered
- Demonstrates senior-level thinking to potential employers/clients
- Examples: "Why I chose X over Y", "Architecture decisions for Z"

**3. Build-in-Public / Journey Posts**
- Document what you're learning and building
- Share progress, failures, and lessons
- Creates authentic connection with readers
- Examples: "Building my blog with Next.js", "Lessons from migrating to Cloudflare"

**4. Deep Dives on Your Stack**
- Become known for expertise in specific technologies
- Next.js, Cloudflare, PostHog, TypeScript
- Attracts relevant job opportunities in these areas

### Quality Standards

- Show your thinking process, not just solutions
- Be honest about what you don't know
- Update posts when you learn better approaches
- Prioritize depth over quantity

### Visibility Checklist

- [ ] SEO: Clear titles, meta descriptions, structured headings
- [ ] Share on Twitter/X, LinkedIn when published
- [ ] Cross-post to Dev.to/Hashnode with canonical links
- [ ] Engage in relevant communities (don't just drop links)

### Conversion Elements

- [ ] Clear About page with professional background
- [ ] Contact information easily accessible
- [ ] Links to GitHub and LinkedIn
- [ ] "Available for opportunities" statement (if applicable)
