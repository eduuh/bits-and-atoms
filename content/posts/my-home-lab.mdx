A Home Lab is a playground for learning, testing, and hosting your own services. Here is a tour of my setup.

## The Hardware

- **Compute:** A cluster of Lenovo Tiny PCs (Project TinyMiniMicro). They are power-efficient, quiet, and powerful enough for most tasks.
- **Storage:** A custom-built NAS running TrueNAS Scale for reliable ZFS storage.
- **Networking:** Managed PoE switches to power cameras and access points.

## The Software Stack

I run Proxmox VE as my hypervisor. It allows me to spin up VMs and LXC containers easily.

## What I Host

- **Media:** Plex/Jellyfin, the *arr stack.
- **Productivity:** Nextcloud, Paperless-ngx.
- **Development:** Gitea, Jenkins, and this blog's staging environment.
